# Repository instructions

- Maintain exactly one installable skill: `skills/show-your-receipts/`.
- Treat `SKILL.md`, referenced policies, templates and helper behavior as one contract.
- Preserve the strict core-journal gate, publication-year coverage and rejection
  of unverifiable evidence. Do not add a bypass flag.
- Every passage and context anchor needs actual section/paragraph attribution.
- Screen abstracts first; trace borrowed claims recursively to the actual origin.
  Preserve every citation edge and PDF. No section automatically proves ownership.
  Unresolved lineage or a final cited claim/hypothesis must block export.
- Preserve original images and generate underlines from matched PDF glyphs.
- Final research delivery is one fixed minimal illustrated Word report and its
  complete evidence ZIP. Do not offer format selection or add covers, TOCs,
  decoration or free-form extra sections.
- Never label an automated review or successful structural check as a guarantee
  of scientific truth.
- Keep English before Chinese in human/agent documentation.
- Use MIT-compatible dependencies and pinned, reviewed versions.
- Keep research PDFs, screenshots, output packets, credentials and caches out of git.
  Documentation may include original SVG diagrams and the explicitly labeled
  synthetic Word layout preview at `docs/images/word-layout-demo.png`.
  This exception never admits real paper screenshots or private documents.
- For substantive behavior changes, run the repository validator and adversarial tests.
- Do not publish a release without passing CI.

## 中文约束

保持单一可安装 Skill、核心期刊准入、发表年份核验、原句匹配、章节定位、
上下文与反证复核。禁止添加跳过验证的开关。英文在前、中文在后。
先看摘要，逐层溯源并保留引用链和原文；章节不能单独证明归属，转引论断
或溯源未完成须阻止导出。
研究文件、私人数据和凭证不得进入版本库；发布前必须通过检查。
文档可包含原创 SVG 说明图和明确标注合成测试的 Word 版式示例，
仅限 `docs/images/word-layout-demo.png`，不允许真实论文截图或私人文稿。
最终固定交付一份极简图文 Word 和原文 ZIP，不让用户自由选格式，
不添加封面、目录、装饰或额外分析。
